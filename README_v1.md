# How Are Singapore's SMEs Thinking About AI Governance and Safety?

**Maria Singh** — Lead Researcher, AI Governance & Cybersecurity
IT595 Master's Capstone in Cybersecurity Management, Purdue Global University · Faculty Advisor: Dr Ash Luitel

This capstone explores how five to eight Singapore SMEs consider AI governance and safety when adopting or exploring AI. Participants will be recruited through the SAIA AI Discovery Clinic, with SCCCI included only if formally confirmed. Twelve supplemental questions will explore existing practices, barriers, and support needs. The findings will provide a small-sample snapshot that may inform a future AI governance support service offered by SAIA.

**Guiding research question:** To what extent do SMEs consider AI governance and safety when exploring AI adoption, what barriers affect their ability to address these issues, and what practical support would help them move forward?

## Frameworks synthesized

Each source plays a distinct role rather than being treated as interchangeable:

- **NIST AI RMF** — the overarching risk-management structure (Govern, Map, Measure, Manage)
- **NIST CSF 2.0** — a common cybersecurity outcome language across organizations of any size
- **ISO/IEC 42001** — the formal, certifiable management-system anchor
- **OWASP GenAI LLM Top 10** — the application-security threat lens for LLM-powered systems
- **AI Verify / IMDA** — Singapore's voluntary testing framework and Model AI Governance guidance for generative and agentic AI
