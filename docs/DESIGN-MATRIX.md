# SME AI Governance & Cybersecurity Design Matrix

This is the interview instrument behind the pilot: [`SME_AI_Governance_Cybersecurity_Design_Matrix.xlsx`](../SME_AI_Governance_Cybersecurity_Design_Matrix.xlsx).

## Structure

| Sheet | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `Start Here` | How to use the workbook, six-step interview process |
| `Question Guide` | The 12 interview questions, one per row |
| `SME-01` … `SME-10` | One interview tab per SME in the cohort |
| `Data Log` | Auto-linked flat record — one row per SME per question. Do not edit directly. |

| `Sources` | APA references and framework traceability (S1–S6) |

## The ten domains

Twelve questions (Q01–Q12) map onto ten scored domains, plus one unscored qualitative question:

1. AI use context
2. Accountability and governance (Q02, Q03)
3. Data protection
4. Third-party management
5. Access and integration security
6. Human oversight
7. Testing and assurance
8. Monitoring and change
9. Incident response
10. People and policy

Q12 — *Barriers and support* — is asked of every SME but is not scored. It's the qualitative question that explains why a domain scored the way it did, and what practical support would move it forward.

## Scoring model

Each domain is coded from four cumulative evidence checks gathered during the interview:

- **Practice** — does it exist?
- **Documented** — is it written down?
- **Implemented** — is it operating?
- **Reviewed** — is it periodically checked?

These combine into a 0–3 maturity score:

| Score | Maturity | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Absent | No reported practice or evidence. |
| 1 | Informal / ad hoc | A reported practice exists but is not both documented and implemented. |
| 2 | Documented & implemented | Recorded and operating, but routine review was not demonstrated. |
| 3 | Managed & reviewed | Recorded, operating, monitored, and periodically reviewed. |

A blank or "Unknown" required check returns *Incomplete* or *Needs review* rather than a silent zero.

## Interview process

1. **Prepare** — review the question, the control it measures, and the expected evidence.
2. **Interview** — ask conversationally; the four checks are never read out as a checklist.
3. **Code evidence** — after listening, code each check as Yes / No / Unknown.
4. **Record basis** — note the strongest evidence basis and confidence level. No confidential documents are requested or stored.
5. **Review score** — the 0–3 score calculates automatically.
6. **Analyze** — SME- and domain-level patterns come from `Results`; barrier/support themes come from every SME's Q12.

## Reporting rule

Results are reported in aggregate. **Individual SMEs are not ranked or identified publicly.**
